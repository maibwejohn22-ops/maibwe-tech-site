About

A technology company focused on real needs

Applications John Maibwe (AJM) is a Québec technology development company. It helps businesses, organizations and individuals through their digital transformation — from the first idea to a tool that works.

AJM designs web and mobile apps, custom software and artificial intelligence solutions built around real needs: making a community more visible, helping an organization communicate better, automating repetitive work or turning an idea into a real digital product.

We don’t just build nice websites. We design practical solutions that make everyday work easier. Small businesses, churches, schools, associations, entrepreneurs: we speak your language, not just the language of technology. Every project starts with one simple question — what would truly save you time?
