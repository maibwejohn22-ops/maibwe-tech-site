Our commitments

  • Hosted in Québec

    Your apps run on our own server, at OVHcloud in Beauharnois. You know where your data is.

    OVHcloud · Beauharnois

  • Encrypted backups

    Your data is copied regularly, and those copies are encrypted: unreadable to anyone without the key.

    PostgreSQL · encrypted copies

  • HTTPS everywhere

    Every exchange between your users and the server is encrypted. Browsers are even instructed to use only the secure version.

    HTTPS · HSTS

  • Québec’s Law 25: your data stays yours

    We collect only what is strictly necessary, and we never sell it. A named person is in charge of protecting it: John Maibwe.
