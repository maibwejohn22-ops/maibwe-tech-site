How much does a website or an app cost?

Every project is unique: the price depends on the number of pages or screens, the features and your needs. We offer a free assessment and a clear quote before any commitment — no surprises along the way.

How long will my project take?

A business website usually takes a few weeks. A mobile app or custom software takes longer, depending on the scope of the project: we give you a realistic timeline from the start.

Do you offer artificial intelligence solutions?

Yes. We bring in artificial intelligence where it delivers real value: assistants, document sorting and drafting, search across your information, task automation. We explain clearly what the AI does with your data before anything is put in place.

Do you handle hosting and the domain name?

Yes. We take care of the domain name, hosting on our own server in Québec, business email and going live. You have nothing technical to manage: that’s our job.

Can my website or app evolve after launch?

Absolutely — that’s our whole philosophy. We keep improving your tools based on your feedback, and we also take over projects built by others to fix or extend them.

Do you work with organizations outside Québec?

Yes. Applications John Maibwe is based in Québec and also serves DR Congo and francophone Africa: meetings take place remotely, and follow-up happens online.
