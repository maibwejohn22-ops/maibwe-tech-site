  1. 01 / 04

    Your idea.

    From the outside, an app is a single thing: an icon on a phone, an address on the web. It all starts with what you want to offer your members, your students or your customers.

  2. 02 / 04

    The five layers.

    1. 01

      Screen iPhone · Android · Web

      What your members see and touch, on a phone or a computer.

    2. 02

      AI Assistants · automation

      AI wherever it saves you time: drafting, sorting, answering.

    3. 03

      Features API · business logic

      Your rules, turned into actions: posting an announcement, receiving a request.

    4. 04

      Data PostgreSQL · backups

      Your information, neatly organized and protected, with encrypted backups.

    5. 05

      Server OVHcloud · Docker

      Our own server, hosted in Beauharnois, Québec.

  3. 03 / 04

    The launch.

    When everything is ready, the layers come back together as a single app. We publish it where your users will find it.
